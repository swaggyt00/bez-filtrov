# v0.7 Seduction Rebuild — Design Spec

## Goal
Rebuild the app around the approved content quality bar and the chosen Seduction visual direction, without adding unrequested product complexity.

## Scope
This release changes four things only:
1. Replace the current card corpus with a new 480-card authored deck.
2. Make `afterdark` support 2–6 players instead of 3–6.
3. Add random duration tokens for temporary effects such as clothing swaps/removal and other multi-turn rules.
4. Redesign the UI around the approved Seduction direction: black/red, adult, cinematic, minimal, card-first.

The existing role-aware selection engine, skip/re-roll behavior, persistence model, and scenario/heat structure remain unless a change is required by the items above.

## Content model
The new deck contains exactly 480 cards:
- 4 scenarios: `couple`, `sex`, `party`, `afterdark`
- 3 heat levels: `light`, `hot`, `hard`
- 2 card types: `truth`, `dare`
- 20 authored cards per scenario × heat × type bucket

### Editorial standard
The approved style is the final 30-card sample from chat:
- short, spoken Russian;
- one clear idea per card;
- no abstract filler or AI/corporate wording;
- no pseudo-psychology that needs explanation;
- a Truth must ask one understandable question;
- a Dare must state exactly what to do;
- if another person/object is involved, the card must identify whom/what;
- if an effect persists, the card must state when it ends;
- if the meaning needs a separate explanation, rewrite or remove the card;
- heat increases the content intensity, not the verbosity.

Forbidden patterns include vague formulations such as `темп`, `динамика`, `сценарий для тебя`, `что для тебя значит`, or dangling `это/такое/этот вариант` when the referent is not established in the same card.

### Scenario boundaries
- `couple`: connection, humor, habits, conflicts, vulnerability, kissing/flirting; no Sex-mode explicit actions.
- `sex`: attraction, preferences, direct sexual conversation, and bounded consensual adult actions appropriate to the selected heat.
- `party`: humor, social choices, flirting, group reactions, alcohol, pair/group challenges.
- `afterdark`: 2–6 players; late-night party chaos, alcohol, clothing, embarrassment, flirting, and bold social tasks; it does not become the Sex mode.

## Random duration effects
Temporary-effect cards may contain a semantic duration slot instead of hardcoding near-duplicate copies.

Supported outcomes:
- `1 круг`
- `2 круга`
- `3 круга`
- `до конца игры`

Heat weighting:
- `light`: short effects only; no `до конца игры`.
- `hot`: mainly 1–3 rounds.
- `hard`: 2–3 rounds are common; `до конца игры` is possible but less frequent than finite durations.

The rendered card text must always include the resolved duration. A player must never see an unresolved placeholder.

## Player-count behavior
- `couple`: keep current release rules.
- `sex`: keep current release rules.
- `party`: keep current 3–6 rule.
- `afterdark`: change to 2–6.

Cards that require three or more participants must be ineligible when only two people are playing Afterdark. Two-player Afterdark must still have enough valid Truth and Dare cards at all three heat levels.

## Visual direction — Seduction
The chosen design direction is the first concept shown in chat.

### Visual principles
- near-black background;
- deep red / crimson as the primary accent;
- warm off-white text;
- subtle red glow, gradients and shadows rather than bright neon;
- cinematic, adult and bold, but not porn-site, casino or generic AI aesthetic;
- minimal chrome;
- the current card is the dominant object on the game screen;
- readable at phone width first, desktop second.

### Screens
The release must restyle the existing flow rather than invent unrelated screens:
- age gate;
- setup / scenario selection;
- player setup;
- heat selection;
- turn choice (`Правда` / `Действие`);
- revealed card;
- notices/errors/resume state.

### Card screen
The revealed card is the visual center. It should show only the information needed to play:
- scenario / heat / type context;
- the card copy;
- current player / target when relevant;
- reroll/skip/next controls.

Avoid decorative navigation that implies features the app does not have (for example fake `Статистика` or `Колоды` tabs).

## Interaction and motion
Motion is restrained:
- short reveal transition for a new card;
- tactile active/pressed states;
- subtle glow/scale on the selected mode or heat;
- no constant pulsing, excessive particles or casino-like effects.

Existing decorative sound may remain but must never block gameplay.

## Validation
Before merge, v0.7 must prove:
- exactly 480 authored cards and 20 per bucket;
- no exact duplicate card text;
- editorial lint catches banned vague/AI-style patterns;
- Dare copy has no unresolved target/duration placeholders;
- Afterdark accepts 2 players and rejects counts outside 2–6;
- group-only Afterdark cards cannot appear for 2 players;
- randomized durations obey heat-specific allowed outcomes;
- all existing role/gender eligibility tests remain green;
- production build succeeds.

## Out of scope for v0.7
- PostHog/analytics instrumentation;
- accounts/backend/cloud sync;
- adding new standalone game modes such as Never Have I Ever or Wheel;
- rankings, scores or leaderboards;
- generated AI card copy at runtime.
